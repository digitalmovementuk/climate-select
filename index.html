Is there an external unit?

No external ducting or condenser unit. We focus on providing integrated air-conditioning systems, which contain all components internally without requiring outdoor condensers or external refrigerant pipes, offering discreet, quiet solutions for homes with restrictions on outdoor units.

Does it heat as well as cool?

Year round temperature control: our units cool and heat the room all year round. Reduced condensation.

How much does it cost?

We give you indicative costs before undertaking our surveys. We will discuss your circumstances, advise a solution and provide an upfront project cost.

Is the home survey free?

FREE (conditions apply) on-site survey. We undertake a comprehensive on-site survey of all projects.

Do I have to use your installation team?

If you wish to save costs or undertake the work more quickly, there is no obligation to use our installation teams.

Which properties is it suited to?

Ideally suited to: homes, flats, hotels, care homes, modular buildings, garden rooms, residential high rise, apartments, listed buildings.
