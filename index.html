A conventional split system

Bolts this to the outside of your home.

An outdoor condenser on a bracket, pipework in trunking down the wall, a drain. In a flat, a listed home or a conservation area it is often not allowed at all.

A Climate Select system

Puts nothing there.

Every part of the system sits inside the room. The outside wall stays exactly as it was, apart from two small vents.

Two vents, nothing else

Air in, air out. The rest stays inside.

Two discreet vents through the wall let the unit draw air in and send it out. Cooling in summer, heating in winter, from one slim unit in the room.

Every recommendation starts with a survey of your home

A system that works well in one room can underperform in another. Your surveyor looks at the things that decide what will work for your property.

  • Glazing quality and window area
  • Solar gain and which way the room faces
  • Insulation levels
  • Ambient noise
  • Room size and how it will be used
  • Outdoor access and installation logistics

Questions people ask before they book

Short answers. If yours is not here, call 01422 485563 and ask.

Do I need an outdoor unit?

No. Our self-contained systems house all components inside, so there is no outdoor condenser unit and no external refrigerant pipework. Two small vents through the wall are all that shows outside.

Can the system heat as well as cool?

Yes. Our systems are reverse-cycle units that provide cooling in summer and heating in winter.

How much does it cost?

We give an indicative equipment cost before you instruct a survey. After the survey, and before you order, your surveyor confirms the project cost.

Is the home survey free?

We offer a free on-site survey, subject to conditions. We explain the conditions when you book.

Do I have to use your installation team?

No. Many customers use their own local tradespeople. Your surveyor confirms what your installer needs during the survey.

Which properties is it suited to?

Flats, garden rooms, offices, conservatories, granny annexes, listed buildings, and properties with planning or lease restrictions on external works. Each home is surveyed before we recommend a system.
