Two ways to get your system

Both options include delivery in around 10 days. Choose the one that fits your timetable and budget.

Supply only

We supply, you install

Your system is delivered in around 10 days. You or your own tradesperson install it, following the instructions supplied with the units.

Best if you want to reduce costs or work to your own timetable.

Supply and install

We supply and install

Your system is delivered in around 10 days, and our installation team arrives as scheduled. The installer gives you a full demonstration of how to use it.

Best if you would rather hand over the whole job.

From survey to handover

What happens between booking a survey and sitting in a comfortable room.

  1. 1

    Survey

    Your surveyor confirms the recommended model, the fitting position and the installation logistics, and marks where the two cores will go.

  2. 2

    Order and delivery

    Once you order, your goods arrive in around 10 days.

  3. 3

    Installation

    Two cores through the wall, the unit mounted inside, two vents outside. Our team fits the system on the scheduled day, or your own tradesperson follows the supplied instructions.

  4. 4

    Handover

    If installation is included, the installer shows you how to use the system on the day.

No outdoor works, and no refrigerant pipework across the wall

Because every component sits inside the unit, the installation avoids the outdoor work that makes traditional systems slow and costly.

  • No outdoor condenser unit to fit to the building
  • No external refrigerant pipework across the wall
  • No external ducting to route or make good
  • Follow the supplied instructions issued with the units

Installation questions, answered

Short answers. If yours is not here, call 01422 485563 and ask.

Can my own tradesperson install it?

Many customers use their own local tradespeople. Your surveyor confirms what your installer needs for the recommended model before any work starts.

How long does delivery take?

Goods arrive in around 10 days, for both the supply only and the supply and install options.

Do I have to use your installation team?

No. There is no obligation to use our installation teams.

Is installation difficult?

Our systems are designed for ease of installation. Installation follows the instructions issued with the units, so please read them before work starts.
