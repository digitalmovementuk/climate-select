Professional Assessments with Transparent Pricing

Our in-depth survey covers all critical property factors — room size, glazing, solar gain, insulation levels, expected usage, noise, outdoor access, and installation logistics — ensuring precise system suitability.

  1. 1

    Glazing

    Glazing quality, and how much of the room is glass.

  2. 2

    Solar Gain

    Which way the room faces and how much sun comes in through the day.

  3. 3

    Insulation

    Insulation levels in the walls, roof and floor.

  4. 4

    Ambient Noise

    The noise around the property, so the unit is positioned to suit the room.

  5. 5

    Room Size and Expected Usage

    The size of the room and how it will be used, to select the best system.

  6. 6

    Outdoor Access and Installation Logistics

    Outdoor access and installation logistics, confirmed by our surveyor before you order.
